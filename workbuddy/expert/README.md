# Teable Assistant

A WorkBuddy expert that works with your [Teable](https://teable.ai) projects: it queries and
analyzes records, creates, updates and deletes them, manages tables and fields, and builds
Teable apps on your machine.

When you summon it, WorkBuddy asks you to connect the Teable MCP server and sign in with your
Teable account. Everything it does runs as you, within your own permissions.

Source: https://github.com/teableio/agent-skills
